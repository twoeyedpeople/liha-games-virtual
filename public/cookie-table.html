Last updated: 3 August 2026

Why this matters: this training hub uses your browser's local storage to remember who you are during the event and to save your progress and score as you complete each activity. If you select Reject on the cookie banner, this site will not be able to save or track your score and progress — you can still take part, but nothing will be remembered between activities or reported back to your session's leaderboard.

This page lists the cookies and local storage this site sets. It is separate from LinkedIn's own Cookie Policy, which covers linkedin.com itself.
